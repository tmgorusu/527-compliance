# 8872 Style Guide and Instructions

Extracted from the client workbook tab “Instructions and Style Guide”.
This is a **client process document**, not IRS law. Federal legal rules are cited from IRS Instructions for Form 8872 and the official XSD.

## Fonts / presentation

- Arial 11 point and **ALL CAPS** except for header rows.
- Schedule A transactions: no dollar signs and no decimals.
- Schedule B transactions: no dollar signs.

## Schedule A headers

Contributor Name, Address Line 1, Address Line 2, City, State, ZIP 5, ZIP 4, Contributor Employer, Contributor Occupation, Contribution Date, Contribution Amount, Aggregate Contributions YTD

## Schedule B headers

Recipient Name, Address Line 1, Address Line 2, City, State, ZIP 5, ZIP 4, Recipient Employer, Recipient Occupation, Expenditure Date, Expenditure Amount, Expenditure Purpose

## Schedule A data

- All contributions above $200 must be disclosed unless otherwise noted by a unanimous consent agreement between the fundraisers and Precision.
- All contributions below $200 must be added and denoted **AGGREGATE BELOW THRESHOLD** with the client's office address being reported.

## Schedule B data

- All expenditures above $500 must be disclosed unless otherwise noted by a unanimous consent agreement between the fundraisers and Precision.
- All contributions below $500 must be added and denoted **WITHHELD** with the client's office address being reported.  
  *(Workbook wording says “contributions” here; context is Schedule B expenditures.)*
- The only unapplied cash bill payment expenditures that can be disclosed are the transactions that are not equal to zero.
- The final unapplied cash bill payment expenditure must be factored into the withheld.

## Profit and Loss

- P&L should correspond with the correct quarter for filing the 8872.
- Schedule A and B totals must match total revenue and expenses in the P&L.

## Vendor totals

- Only disclose data for vendors over $500 unless otherwise noted.

## Raw vendor data

- Download raw expenditures in QBO from the P&L, not the expenditures tab.
- Highlight unapplied cash bill payment expenditures in RED.
- Delete any unapplied cash bill payment entries that have zero balances.

## Filing Access

- Do **not** change the Filing Access tab unless necessary; contact the designated owner first.
- **Tool rule:** never read, store, log, transmit, or export portal credentials. Humans upload output files.

## Unanimous consent

- Exceptions are provided only via optional `exceptions.csv`. Never assume one exists.
